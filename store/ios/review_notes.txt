APP PURPOSE
Kajiri (カジリ) is a task manager and calendar for people who put things off, especially people with ADHD traits. The user "throws" a rough note, and Kajiri, a beaver character powered by Anthropic's Claude, splits it into steps of 15 minutes or less, each with an owner (me / Kajiri / ask someone / waiting). Steps Kajiri can do (a draft, a checklist, a short research note) are written in the background. Home shows today's events and places up to 3 steps in the free time between them.

TEST DEVICE
iPhone 18 Pro Max simulator (iOS 27.2). The app is iPhone only.

SETUP (no account, no login)
1. Launch the app. Home shows Kajiri and two buttons: "最初の丸太を投げる" (Throw your first log) and "先にカレンダーの予定を並べる" (Show my calendar first).
2. Calendar (optional): tap "先にカレンダーの予定を並べる" and allow Full Access. The app also works without it.
3. Throw a log: tap "+" (or "最初の丸太を投げる"), type a note, e.g. "年末調整の書類、放置してる。締め切りは11月20日", and tap "カジリに渡す" (Give it to Kajiri). In a few seconds the steps appear. Tap "これでいこう" (Let's go with this). The app then asks for notification permission.
4. Today's steps sit in the free time on Home. Tap one to open "この1本だけ" (Just this one) with the first physical motion. Tap "できた" (Done) or "あとで" (Later). "あとで" can have Kajiri split it smaller.
5. Kajiri's work: cards labeled "カジリが運んできた" (Kajiri brought this) open the draft. "コピーする" (Copy) / "送る・使う" (Send or use) / "受け取った" (Received).
6. Month view: tap the big date at the top left. "カジリに見てもらう" (Ask Kajiri to look) sends the next 2 weeks of event titles, times and places (only when tapped); Kajiri suggests events that need preparation.
7. Weekly review: tap the "丸太" bar at the bottom of Home, then "週のふりかえり" (Weekly review), then "カジリにふりかえってもらう".
8. Share text or a URL to "Kajiri" from Safari or Mail; it opens in the throw sheet. Siri: "Kajiriに投げる".

DEMO ACCOUNT
Not needed. There is no sign-up or login.

IN-APP PURCHASE
None. The app is free with no ads.

EXTERNAL SERVICES
- Anthropic Claude API (model claude-opus-5-5): breaks notes into steps, writes Kajiri's drafts (with web search for facts), suggests preparation for events, and writes the weekly review.
- Firebase Cloud Functions (asia-northeast1): our relay server to the Claude API. The app holds no API key; the relay does not store or log content.
- Firebase App Check with App Attest: the relay accepts calls only from the genuine app.
Tasks are stored only on the device. Calendar events are not copied.

AI-GENERATED CONTENT
All of Kajiri's suggestions and drafts are generated by AI. The app says so on screen ("カジリの提案は AI（Claude）が作ります。送る前・決める前に、中身を確かめてください。" = Kajiri's suggestions are made by AI; check them before sending or deciding). Drafts are never sent automatically; the user copies or shares them. Facts only the user knows are left as blanks such as "（ここに一言）".

PERMISSIONS
- Calendars (Full Access): to show events on the day timeline and place today's steps in the free time. Optional.
- Notifications: at most one notice at a time: the first step in the morning, the evening before a deadline, and the Monday review.

REGIONAL BEHAVIOR
Functions consistently across all regions. The UI is in Japanese.
